How to read this

Wireflow stretches top → bottom so lanes can breathe: Discord rails on top, Conductor as its own orchestration rail, specialists left → right in the middle, the Queue as a separate bus under them, shared-brain stores on the bottom. Queue and Conductor are not specialist agents in the main path. Arrows are real handoffs (solid orange stages · purple Discord · blue stores · red kill). Canvas fits your screen — zoom/pan as needed. Hover a stage to highlight only its wires; click anything for the detail drawer. Sequence is the same handoffs as a timeline. Legend spells out every symbol.

Esc or click the backdrop → closes the drawer.
